          📚 A to Z Explanation: Why & How We Use SNS and SQS Together
        

        

          Understanding the cloud architectural patterns that make enterprise serverless systems resilient to spikes and failure.
        

      


      

        
        
        

          

            📢 1. Amazon SNS (Pub/Sub)
          

          

            Role: Publisher / Subscriber broadcast hub.
          

          

            When an order arrives, the ingestion service doesn't know (or care) who needs this event. It simply publishes a single OrderCreated CloudEvent to SNS.
          

          

            Fan-out benefit: Multiple queues (Order Processing, Analytics, Customer SMS Notifications, Fraud Detection) can subscribe independently without changing a single line of API code!
          

        


        
        

          

            📥 2. Amazon SQS (Buffering)
          

          

            Role: Rate-limiting shock absorber & persistent queue.
          

          

            If 10,000 customers place orders in 1 second during a flash sale, SNS pushes all messages into SQS. SQS safely buffers them.
          

          

            Backpressure benefit: Downstream databases and workers are never overwhelmed. Lambda pulls messages at a controlled rate (e.g., batches of 10), preventing DynamoDB throttling.
          

        


        
        

          

            🛡️ 3. DLQ & Idempotency
          

          

            Role: Zero data loss & duplicate prevention.
          

          

            SQS guarantees at-least-once delivery. A network blip might re-deliver a message. The worker uses DynamoDB conditional writes to discard duplicate events safely.
          

          

            DLQ benefit: If a poison-pill payload fails 3 times (maxReceiveCount = 3), it is moved to the DLQ instead of blocking the entire queue forever.
